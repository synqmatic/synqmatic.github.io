How I work

Three habits apply to every track.

Evidence beats demos.

A demo shows one good run. A note here shows how many cases were tried, how many passed, and what the failures had in common.

Use a model where judgment is needed.

Plain code does most of the work in most systems. A model earns a place at the steps that are truly fuzzy, and nowhere else.

A person stays accountable.

People set the goals, approve what commits, and own the outcome. Systems are built to hand back to them when they are unsure.
