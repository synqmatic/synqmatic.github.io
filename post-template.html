How to use this template

Orange brackets are placeholders. Dashed boxes are prompts for you and come out before publishing. Copy the page, fill each section, and delete any section the post does not need. A post about only the audit can skip evidence and numbers. Dated baselines and predictions are what make the later posts credible, so write them early.
