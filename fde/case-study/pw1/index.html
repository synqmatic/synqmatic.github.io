Primary Workflow 1: new product reviews

Posts about the first primary workflow in the AI FDE live case study: how Team T walks its customers through a new product release. Primary workflows are the work the team actually needs done. Auditing one shows which smaller base workflows it depends on. Part of an anonymized run of the process with a real team. Nothing here names a company, customer or product.

Four parts so far: the audit, the judgment, the eval design and the build plan. Nothing has been built or measured yet.
