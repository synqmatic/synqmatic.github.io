This track in practice

Six lifecycle stages, with AI in a defined role at each.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Turn a vague request into requirements specific enough that an agent could build from them.

  2. 2

    Design

    Use AI to explore options and write down the trade-offs. A person chooses.

  3. 3

    Build

    Coding agents draft. Small changes, a clear scope and tests in place first.

  4. 4

    Verify

    Review and testing carry the risk. Generated code is read like any other code.

  5. 5

    Release

    Pipelines, approvals and rollbacks stay deterministic and logged.

  6. 6

    Operate

    Monitor, triage and document. Incidents feed the next plan.

At every stage the questions are the same: who does the work, what checks it, and who signs off.
